## Why The Old Hunters Is Worth Unlocking

The Old Hunters is the only expansion FromSoftware ever released for Bloodborne, and it hides some of the game's best bosses, strangest weapons, and most unsettling areas behind a trigger the game never explains. That silence is why so many players go searching for **Bloodborne DLC how to access** answers only after they have already cleared half of Yharnam. The good news is that the unlock chain is short — three items, one boss, and one very specific patch of cobblestone.

Knowing **Bloodborne DLC how to access** early also protects you from a common trap: the expansion scales with your level and your New Game+ cycle. If you finish the main story first and roll into NG+, the Hunter's Nightmare gets harder with you. Unlocking it at the right moment keeps the difficulty curve fair and your build relevant.

## What The Old Hunters Actually Contains

The expansion adds three connected areas, four main bosses (plus one infamous optional fight), and a pile of trick weapons that carry back into the base game. It is not a side story — it is the game's best content, and it explains a lot of Bloodborne's lore.

| Area | Main Boss | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Hunter's Nightmare | Ludwig, the Accursed; Laurence, the First Vicar (optional) | Opening zone, most new weapons, two of the toughest fights in the game |
| Research Hall | Living Failures; Lady Maria of the Astral Clocktower | Platforming, puzzles, and the expansion's best duel |
| Fishing Hamlet | Orphan of Kos | The final boss and the hardest encounter FromSoftware shipped in 2015 |

Community reports consistently place The Old Hunters among the hardest content in the base game, so treat it as a late-game detour rather than a victory lap. The expansion is sold as a standalone download, and it is also bundled in the Bloodborne: The Old Hunters Edition (often called the Game of the Year Edition). If your copy came from a subscription service or a plain retail disc, double-check whether the expansion is actually installed before you start hunting for triggers.

## Prerequisites: What You Need Before the Nightmare Opens

You do not need to finish the game. You do not need to reach the Blood Moon. You need exactly one boss kill and one key item.

| Requirement | Where | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| The Old Hunters installed | PlayStation Store or disc bundle | Without the files, the trigger does nothing |
| Vicar Amelia defeated | Grand Cathedral, Cathedral Ward | Unlocks the altar interaction that changes the world state |
| Skull on the altar touched | Behind Amelia's arena | Shifts the world from day to night and flags the DLC as available |
| Eye of a Blood-Drunk Hunter | Hunter's Dream, near the workshop | The key item that opens the Hunter's Nightmare |
| Access to the Cathedral Ward plaza | Oedon Chapel exit | The physical location where the teleport happens |

That is the whole checklist. Players who already beat Vicar Amelia months ago usually only need the last two rows — the world state is already set, and the Eye may already be sitting in their inventory.

## Bloodborne DLC How to Access: The Six-Step Unlock

Here is the full sequence, in order. Each step takes under five minutes once you know where to stand.

| Step | Location | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grand Cathedral | Defeat Vicar Amelia |
| 2 | Grand Cathedral altar | Interact with the skull to trigger nightfall |
| 3 | Hunter's Dream | Pick up the Eye of a Blood-Drunk Hunter from the messenger by the workshop |
| 4 | Cathedral Ward | Walk to the plaza outside Oedon Chapel |
| 5 | Cathedral Ward plaza | Stand in the open and let the Amygdala's hand grab you |
| 6 | Hunter's Nightmare | Light the lamp and start exploring |

**Step 1–2:** Vicar Amelia is the third mandatory boss in the game, so most players reach her within the first several hours. After she falls, walk to the far end of the arena and interact with the golden skull on the altar. A cutscene plays and the sky over Cathedral Ward turns from afternoon to night. This is the flag that tells the game you are ready for the expansion.

**Step 3:** Fast travel back to the Hunter's Dream and look near the workshop steps. A messenger holds out the Eye of a Blood-Drunk Hunter. Community reports describe it appearing only after the skull interaction, so if you do not see it, go back and touch the skull again.

**Step 4–6:** Return to Cathedral Ward and head out toward the plaza near Oedon Chapel. Stand in the middle of the open courtyard and wait. An Amygdala clinging to the building will reach down, grab you, and drop you in the Hunter's Nightmare. Light the lamp, and you are in.

### Where Exactly Is the Amygdala Grab Spot?

This is the step that stops most people, and it is the number one reason players give up on **Bloodborne DLC how to access** searches. The trigger is a small invisible zone in the courtyard outside Oedon Chapel, near where the Amygdala's hand sweeps across the ground. Walk slowly into the center of the plaza rather than hugging the walls, and stand still for a second. If you also carry the Tonsil Stone, you may occasionally be pulled to the Lecture Building instead — simply return to the plaza and try again.

### A Note on the Old Hunter Bell

The expansion also adds the Old Hunter Bell, which lets you call NPC Old Hunters into your world as backup. It is not part of the unlock chain, but it is worth grabbing early if you plan to fight Ludwig without a co-op partner.

## Troubleshooting: When the Bloodborne DLC Won't Open

If the nightmare refuses to appear, the cause is almost always one of these five issues.

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No Eye in the Hunter's Dream | Skull not touched, or area not reloaded | Touch the skull, then return via lamp |
| Eye in inventory, nothing happens | Standing outside the trigger zone | Walk to the center of the plaza and stand still |
| Blood Moon already active | Not a problem — the trigger is independent | Head to the plaza anyway |
| Nothing at all on a fresh character | Expansion not installed | Check your library or store purchase |
| Disc copy, DLC bought on another account | Region mismatch | Buy the expansion on the same region store as the disc |

One more warning: do not defeat the final boss before you visit the Hunter's Nightmare. Beating the last encounter pushes you into New Game+, and the expansion will scale up with you. Finish the DLC first, then finish the story.

## Recommended Levels and When to Play

There is no official level requirement, but the community consensus is that the expansion is tuned for a character who has cleared most of the base game. These ranges reflect player experience across the standard difficulty curve.

| Content | Suggested Level | Weapon Upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Hunter's Nightmare (opening area) | 60–70 | +6 to +7 |
| Ludwig, the Accursed | 70–80 | +8 |

Vitality matters more than damage here. Community reports suggest 35–40 Vitality is the comfortable floor, because several DLC attacks can delete a full health bar in one combo. The expansion is also generous with Blood Stone Chunks, which makes it a practical place to finish upgrading a second weapon.

## Your First Hour in the Hunter's Nightmare

Once you arrive, resist the urge to sprint. The opening street is dense with hunters who punish panic rolls.

- **Light the first lamp immediately** — it sits right where you land.
- **Explore the side streets for weapons.** Several of the expansion's new trick weapons are found in the opening zone, and they carry back into the main game.
- **Pick up the Eye Pendant and other key items** to open shortcuts back to the lamp.
- **Do not ring the Beckoning Bell in the open street.** The invaders and hostile hunters in this zone are unusually aggressive.
- **Bank your Blood Echoes before Ludwig.** Spend them on levels or supplies rather than carrying them into a boss arena.

If you are underleveled, go back to the main game, clear a few optional areas, and return. The Hunter's Nightmare is not going anywhere — and unlike most Bloodborne secrets, this one stays unlocked once you have opened it.

For the official walkthrough of the original unlock, the [PlayStation Blog's guide to accessing The Old Hunters](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2015/11/20/how-to-access-bloodborne-the-old-hunters-expansion) is still a useful reference, though the in-game steps have not changed since launch.

## FAQ

**Do I need to beat the whole game to access the Bloodborne DLC?**

No. You only need to defeat Vicar Amelia, touch the skull behind her arena, collect the Eye of a Blood-Drunk Hunter in the Hunter's Dream, and stand in the Cathedral Ward plaza. That is the entire **Bloodborne DLC how to access** chain, and it can be done in the first half of the story.

**Why is there no Eye of a Blood-Drunk Hunter in my Hunter's Dream?**

The item only spawns after you interact with the skull on the Grand Cathedral altar. If you killed Amelia but skipped the altar, the world state never changed. Go back, touch the skull, watch the cutscene, and return to the Dream.

**Can I still play The Old Hunters after starting New Game+?**

Yes, but the expansion scales with your cycle and becomes significantly harder. You will also need to defeat Vicar Amelia again and repeat the unlock steps on that playthrough.

**Does the DLC work on the PS Plus version of Bloodborne?**

Subscription and base-game copies generally include only the original release. Check your library for The Old Hunters add-on, or look for the Old Hunters Edition, which bundles the expansion with the base game.
