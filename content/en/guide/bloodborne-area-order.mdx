Bloodborne rarely tells you where to go next. It drops you into Central Yharnam, hands you a trick weapon, and lets the streets do the talking — which is exactly why a reliable **Bloodborne area order** saves so many first playthroughs. Knowing the intended sequence keeps you from wandering into Yahar'gul underprepared, missing the Healing Church Workshop entirely, or skipping optional zones that hand out some of the game's best weapons and runes. This guide lays out the **Bloodborne area order** step by step, flags which zones are mandatory, and shows where the optional detours fit best so your route stays smooth from the clinic to the final hunt.

## Why the Bloodborne Area Order Matters

Bloodborne looks open, but it is really a series of interlocking corridors, elevators, and locked doors that only make sense in sequence. Wander off too early and you meet enemies scaled for a weapon you have not upgraded yet. Follow the natural progression and every new area feels like a fair fight.

A sensible route also protects content that is easy to lose permanently:

- **NPC questlines** — Eileen the Crow, Arianna, and the Oedon Chapel Dweller all depend on you reaching certain areas in a certain order.
- **Weapon and rune access** — the Tonitrus, the Powder Keg Hunter Badge, and the Rune Workshop Tool all sit behind optional detours.
- **Upgrade materials** — Blood Stone Shards and Twin Blood Stone Shards are far easier to farm once you know where the mid-game shortcuts open.
- **Backtracking** — a planned route means fewer long walks back through cleared streets.

Community reports are consistent on one point: players who follow a rough progression route finish their first run faster and die less than those who improvise. The game's own guidance is minimal, so a written plan is genuinely useful.

## Early Game: Central Yharnam to the Cathedral Ward

Central Yharnam is the tutorial that pretends not to be one. Clear the bonfire mob, open the shortcuts back to the lamp, and decide whether to visit the Great Bridge. The Cleric Beast is optional, but encountering it grants 1 Insight, which unlocks the upper Messenger shop and lets you buy a Small Resonant Bell — worth the detour even if you lose the fight.

Father Gascoigne in the Tomb of Oedon is your first real wall. He punishes panic-rolling, so practice the parry timing on the brick trolls nearby before committing.

| Area | Boss | Before you move on |
|---|---|---|
| Central Yharnam | Father Gascoigne | Buy the Yharnam Hunter Set, open both lamp shortcuts |
| Great Bridge | Cleric Beast (optional) | Bank 1 Insight, grab the Saw Spear if you missed it |
| Cathedral Ward | Vicar Amelia | Talk to the Oedon Chapel Dweller, light the lamp |

At Cathedral Ward you hit the first real fork in the route. You can buy the **Hunter Chief Emblem** from the Lower Bath Messengers for 10,000 Blood Echoes to open the main gate, or you can head into Old Yharnam and earn the same access for free. One caveat from the source material: if you skip the emblem purchase, you cannot summon help for Vicar Amelia until that gate is opened from the other side.

### The Free Alternative: Healing Church Workshop

Beating the Blood-Starved Beast in Old Yharnam opens the previously sealed door beside the Cathedral Ward lamp. Ride the elevator, climb the tower, and instead of entering the tower itself, hug the right side and drop through the hole in the walkway. Follow the rafters down, run the streets below, and pull the lever that opens the gate back into Cathedral Ward. That single lever saves you 10,000 Blood Echoes and is one of the best early returns on investment in the game.

## Mid Game: Old Yharnam, Hypogean Gaol, and Optional Detours

Old Yharnam is entirely optional, yet most veterans insist on it. The area hands you Blood Stone Shards, the Powder Keg Hunter Badge, and a Pthumeru Chalice that unlocks the Chalice Dungeons.

Djura, the hunter on the tower with the Gatling gun, is worth handling carefully. Kill him immediately and you get the badge. Approach him later — after opening the shortcut from Hypogean Gaol and answering "Spare the Beasts" — and you get a gesture on top of the badge. Player experience suggests the second option is the better long-term play.

| Optional area | How to unlock | Main reward | Best time to visit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Old Yharnam | Door left of the Cathedral Ward lamp | Blood-Starved Beast, Pthumeru Chalice, badge | After Gascoigne |
| Healing Church Workshop | Defeat Blood-Starved Beast | Free Cathedral Ward gate lever, Black Church Set | Right after Old Yharnam |
| Hemwick Charnel Lane | Path left of Cathedral Ward | Rune Workshop Tool | Before Vicar Amelia |
| Hypogean Gaol | Let a Snatcher kill you | Tonitrus, Darkbeast Paarl, farming spot | After Blood-Starved Beast |
| Nightmare Frontier | Lecture Building, second floor | Amygdala, Blood Stone Chunks | Mid-to-late game |
| Castle Cainhurst | Cainhurst Summons + obelisk in Hemwick | Martyr Logarius, Evelyn | After Witch of Hemwick |
| Upper Cathedral Ward | Key found later in the campaign | Celestial Emissary, Ebrietas | Late game |

Hypogean Gaol deserves a special mention. Let a Snatcher kill you without fighting back and a cutscene will drop you in this prison district. Community reports put a full clearing run — two Snatchers and two Giant Boars — at roughly 12,000 Blood Echoes, which makes it one of the most efficient early farming loops in the game.

Two more quick detours: the Nightmare Frontier is reached through the Lecture Building's second floor and is famously punishing early, so save it. Castle Cainhurst only opens once the Witch of Hemwick is dead and you hold the Cainhurst Summons, found via the back entrance to Iosefka's Clinic.

## Late Game: Forbidden Woods to the Final Boss

Once Vicar Amelia falls, the Forbidden Woods opens and the game stops being polite. The woods are a maze of identical tree lines, snake bundles, and one very long shortcut back to the lamp.

| Area | Boss | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Forbidden Woods | Shadows of Yharnam | Longest traversal section; open the shortcut gate early |
| Byrgenwerth | Rom, the Vacuous Spider | Killing Rom triggers the Blood Moon |
| Yahar'gul, Unseen Village | The One Reborn | Full area unlocks after the Blood Moon |
| Lecture Building | None | Hub linking to Nightmare of Mensis and Nightmare Frontier |
| Nightmare of Mensis | Micolash, Mergo's Wet Nurse | Winter Lanterns, frenzy resistance required |
| Hunter's Dream | Gehrman, Moon Presence | Endgame trigger |

The Blood Moon is the biggest structural shift in the Bloodborne area order. Enemies respawn differently, new NPCs appear, and previously blocked sections of Yahar'gul become walkable. If you want the true final boss, consume three of the four One Third of Umbilical Cord items before speaking to Gehrman — Arianna's questline is the easiest of the four to miss, so send her to Oedon Chapel early.

## Where The Old Hunters Fits in Your Bloodborne Area Order

The Old Hunters DLC slots in cleanly once Vicar Amelia is defeated. An Amygdala outside Oedon Chapel will grab you and pull you into the Hunter's Nightmare, provided you picked up the Eye of a Blood-Drunk Hunter from the Hunter's Dream.

| Order | DLC area | Boss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hunter's Nightmare | Ludwig, the Accursed | Also home to Laurence, the First Vicar |
| 2 | Research Hall | Living Failures | Feed 3 Brain Fluids to Saint Adeline for the Blood of Adeline |
| 3 | Astral Clocktower | Lady Maria | Opens the path to the Fishing Hamlet |
| 4 | Fishing Hamlet | Orphan of Kos | Hardest fight in the DLC |

Most players treat the DLC as a late-game chapter rather than a mid-game detour, because the damage scaling is a step above the base campaign. If you want the Blood of Adeline and the associated rewards, handle Saint Adeline's Brain Fluid requests in order before you leave the Research Hall.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the correct Bloodborne area order for a first playthrough?

The safest Bloodborne area order is Central Yharnam, Cathedral Ward, Forbidden Woods, Byrgenwerth, Yahar'gul, the Lecture Building, and Nightmare of Mensis, ending in Hunter's Dream. Slot Old Yharnam and Hemwick Charnel Lane in before Vicar Amelia, and save Hypogean Gaol, Nightmare Frontier, Castle Cainhurst, and Upper Cathedral Ward for whenever your build needs a power spike.

### Can you skip areas in Bloodborne?

Yes — Old Yharnam, Hemwick Charnel Lane, Hypogean Gaol, Nightmare Frontier, Castle Cainhurst, and Upper Cathedral Ward are all optional. Skipping them costs you weapons, runes, upgrade materials, and a few NPC questlines, but none of them are required to reach the credits.

### When should I start The Old Hunters?

The DLC becomes available right after you defeat Vicar Amelia, but the difficulty curve assumes a late-game character. Most players tackle it after Nightmare of Mensis or just before the final boss, when their weapon is heavily upgraded and their Blood Vials go further.

### Do Chalice Dungeons belong in the area order?

No. Chalice Dungeons are a separate side system unlocked by the Pthumeru Chalice from the Blood-Starved Beast. They reward gems and upgrade materials that make the main route easier, but they sit outside the standard progression and can be run at any point you feel underpowered.

### Does the order change on New Game Plus?

The geography stays the same, so the same Bloodborne area order applies — but enemies hit harder and you keep your gear. On a second run, most players skip the optional detours they have already cleared and push straight down the critical path.
