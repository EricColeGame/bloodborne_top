Bloodborne hands out its platinum the old-fashioned way — it makes you earn it. Between three mutually exclusive endings, a weapon list that punishes missed questlines, and a chalice dungeon that halves your health bar, the platinum is less a test of reflexes than a test of planning. That's why a good **Bloodborne trophy guide** matters: it tells you what to do first, what you can never undo, and how to avoid an unnecessary third playthrough.

This **Bloodborne trophy guide** is built around one idea — front-load every decision that can lock you out, then back-load the grinding. Do that and the platinum becomes a long but smooth hunt instead of a frustrating cleanup job in New Game Plus.

## Bloodborne Trophy Guide at a Glance

Before you create a character, know what you're signing up for. The numbers below reflect community consensus from trophy-hunting sites and player experience across the base game.

| Detail | What to Expect |
|---|---|
| Platinum difficulty | Around 6/10 (community consensus) |
| Estimated completion time | Roughly 50–80 hours |
| Minimum playthroughs | One, if you back up your save; three otherwise |
| Missable trophies | Yes — NPC questlines, cords, and endings |
| Difficulty settings | None; the game is what it is |
| Online trophies | None in the base game |
| DLC | The Old Hunters has its own separate trophy set |

That "no online trophies" line is genuinely good news. You can chase the platinum entirely offline, and PlayStation Plus is only needed if you want co-op help with a boss — see the [official Bloodborne page on PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/bloodborne/) for platform details.

## The Roadmap: Six Phases to Platinum

Speedrunners aside, most players who follow a structured route finish in two or three sittings per phase. Treat the table below as your itinerary.

| Phase | Where You Are | Key Trophies | Don't Forget |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Early Yharnam | Central Yharnam → Cathedral Ward | Cleric Beast, Father Gascoigne, Blood-starved Beast | Start Eileen's questline |
| 2. Mid game | Forbidden Woods → Byrgenwerth | Shadows of Yharnam, Rom | Grab the Cainhurst Summons |
| 3. Optional branch | Hemwick, Cainhurst, Nightmare Frontier | Witch of Hemwick, Martyr Logarius, Amygdala | Cainhurst weapons and gear |
| 4. Blood Moon | Yahar'gul → Nightmare of Mensis | The One Reborn, Micolash, Mergo's Wet Nurse | Collect umbilical cords |
| 5. Cleanup | Chalice dungeons, Upper Cathedral Ward | Pthumerian Queen, collection trophies | Beast Claw, hunter tools |
| 6. Finale | Hunter's Dream | All three endings | Back up your save first |

Phase 3 is the one people skip and regret. Cainhurst Castle alone hides several weapons tied to the collection trophies, and Hemwick Charnel Lane is where you trigger the Cainhurst invite. Both are optional areas with mandatory rewards for a completionist.

## Missable Trophies: The Stuff You Can't Undo

Bloodborne is generous with checkpoints and stingy with second chances. Community reports consistently flag the same handful of irreversible mistakes.

| Missable | What Locks It | How to Avoid It |
|---|---|---|
| Blade of Mercy (Hunter's Essence) | Eileen's questline fails if she dies | Complete her hunts in order; don't attack her |
| One Third of Umbilical Cord (Arianna) | Arianna must survive and be sent to Oedon Chapel | Never send her to Iosefka's Clinic |
| One Third of Umbilical Cord (Iosefka) | Only reachable before the finale | Enter the clinic from the Forbidden Woods cave |
| Childhood's Beginning | Needs three cords consumed before the last boss | Bank cords early, consume them late |
| All three endings | One ending per playthrough by default | Back up your save (see below) |
| Beast Claw | Chalice dungeon exclusive | Farm it during your Depth 4 run |

The Arianna cord is the one that catches veterans, not newcomers. She has to be alive, in the chapel, and past the Blood Moon — and if you sent her to the clinic instead, that cord is gone for the run.

## The Three Endings and the Save-Backup Trick

Three trophies come from the game's three endings, and only one can be triggered per playthrough. Here's how they break down.

| Ending | Trigger | Trophy | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yharnam Sunrise | Accept Gehrman's offer | Yharnam Sunrise | None — fastest route |
| Honoring Wishes | Refuse, then defeat Gehrman | Honoring Wishes | Beat the first hunter |
| Childhood's Beginning | Consume three cords, refuse, beat Gehrman and the Moon Presence | Childhood's Beginning | Three umbilical cords |

To get all three in a single run, back up your save to PlayStation Plus cloud storage or a USB drive before you speak to Gehrman, then restore it after each ending. Turn off auto-upload first, or your backup will be overwritten the moment the credits roll.

Community reports suggest this trick saves 15–20 hours compared with three full playthroughs — and it's the single biggest shortcut in any Bloodborne trophy guide worth reading.

## Collection Trophies: Weapons, Tools, Gems, and Runes

These are the trophies that separate a casual platinum from a grindy one. None are hard; all are bookkeeping.

| Trophy | Requirement | The Real Obstacle |
|---|---|---|
| Hunter's Essence | Every trick weapon and firearm in the base game | Eileen's Blade of Mercy, Cainhurst gear, Beast Claw |
| Hunter's Craft | All nine hunter tools | A Call Beyond and Choir Bell are easy to walk past |
| Blood Gem Master | A top-tier blood gem | Deep chalice gem farming |
| Boss trophies | One per major and optional boss | Optional bosses like Ebrietas and Martyr Logarius |

The nine hunter tools are Beast Roar, Empty Phantasm Shell, Old Hunter Bone, Tiny Tonitrus, Augur of Ebrietas, Executioner's Gloves, Messenger's Gift, Choir Bell, and A Call Beyond. Tick them off as you go — backtracking through Upper Cathedral Ward for a single item is nobody's idea of fun.

For weapons, keep a written checklist. A few are only sold by the Bath Messengers after you earn specific badges, so visit the shop in the Hunter's Dream after every major boss and buy anything new.

## Chalice Dungeon Trophies and the Defiled Spike

The Pthumerian Queen trophy lives at the bottom of the chalice dungeon chain, and it's the longest single grind in the game. The critical path looks like this:

| Depth | Chalice | Why You're Here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pthumeru | Unlocks the chain; teaches the format |
| 2 | Central Pthumeru | Materials and ritual blood |
| 3 | Lower Pthumeru | Gateway to Depth 4 |
| 4 | Ailing Loran | Bastard of Loran materials, Beast Claw |
| 4 | Defiled Chalice | Mandatory — and brutal |
| 5 | Pthumeru Ihyll | Yharnam, Pthumerian Queen |

The Defiled Chalice halves your maximum HP, which turns normally manageable bosses into one-shot nightmares. The Watchdog of the Old Lords and Amygdala are the two walls most players hit; patient, hit-and-run play beats aggression here.

Two practical notes: Hintertomb is an optional side branch that doesn't lead to the Queen, and chalice dungeons don't scale with New Game Plus, so you can run them at any point. Many players save them for after the main story, when their character is fully built.

## Habits That Save You Hours

- **Read item descriptions.** Bloodborne hides quest triggers in plain sight, and cords, badges, and summons all come with clues.
- **Talk to NPCs twice.** Many dialogue trees only advance on a second interaction.
- **Revisit the Hunter's Dream after every boss.** New shop stock equals progress toward Hunter's Essence.
- **Upgrade one weapon fully rather than five halfway.** Blood Stone Chunks are limited per playthrough.
- **Learn the parry window.** A well-timed firearm shot trivializes several boss trophies that otherwise eat hours.
- **Play offline for the chalice grind** if invasions are slowing you down; nothing in the trophy list requires other players.

Player experience is nearly unanimous on one point: the platinum is far easier than its reputation suggests. Bloodborne rewards preparation more than raw skill, and a planned route removes almost all of the pain.

## FAQ

**How long does a Bloodborne trophy guide run actually take?**
Most community reports land between 50 and 80 hours for the base game platinum, depending on how much you struggle with optional bosses and the Defiled Chalice.

**Can I unlock all three endings in one playthrough?**
Yes. Back up your save before speaking to Gehrman, then restore it after each ending. Turn off cloud auto-upload first so your backup survives.

**Do I need PlayStation Plus for the platinum?**
No. There are no online trophies in the base game. Plus is only useful if you want summoning help for a tough boss.

**Are the chalice dungeon trophies required?**
The Pthumerian Queen trophy is mandatory for the platinum, so yes — you'll need to clear Pthumeru Ihyll at minimum. Following this Bloodborne trophy guide's order keeps that grind from stalling your progress at the very end.
