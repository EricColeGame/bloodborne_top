Three endings, one gate, and a decision you can't take back. Bloodborne's final hour funnels every hunter to the same flower-covered exit in the Hunter's Dream, then splits the story three very different ways based on a single conversation and how many umbilical cords you've consumed. That's why getting Bloodborne endings explained properly matters — pick the wrong option, or skip the cords entirely, and you'll miss the game's most ambitious finale without ever knowing it existed.

Below is a complete Bloodborne endings explained walkthrough: all three routes, the exact requirements, the four umbilical cord locations, the boss fights that separate each finale, and a save-backup trick for seeing every ending in one playthrough.

## Bloodborne Endings Explained: The Three-Way Split

All three endings unlock at the same trigger point. After you defeat **Mergo's Wet Nurse** in the Nightmare of Mensis, return to the Hunter's Dream and approach the gate surrounded by white flowers. Gehrman, the First Hunter, is waiting there, and he offers to end your life and release you from the dream.

Everything branches from that one prompt:

- **Accept his offer** → Ending 1, "Yharnam Sunrise."
- **Refuse the offer and beat Gehrman** → Ending 2, "Honoring Wishes."
- **Consume three One Third of Umbilical Cord items, refuse, beat Gehrman, then beat the Moon Presence** → Ending 3, "Childhood's Beginning."

| Ending | Trophy | Key Requirement | Boss Fights | Final Tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yharnam Sunrise | Yharnam Sunrise | Submit to Gehrman | None | Quiet, ambiguous relief |
| Honoring Wishes | Honoring Wishes | Refuse, win the duel | Gehrman, the First Hunter | Bittersweet, cyclical |
| Childhood's Beginning | Childhood's Beginning | Consume 3 umbilical cords, refuse, win both fights | Gehrman + Moon Presence | Cosmic, triumphant |

Note that the cords are **not** consumed automatically. They sit in your inventory as usable items, and if you never use them, the third ending is simply unavailable — no warning, no prompt.

## Ending 1 — Yharnam Sunrise (Submit Your Life)

This is the shortest route and the one most first-time players stumble into. When Gehrman asks, choose to let him take your life. There is no fight, no skill check, and no penalty.

**Steps:**

1. Defeat Mergo's Wet Nurse in the Nightmare of Mensis.
2. Return to the Hunter's Dream.
3. Speak with Gehrman at the white-flower gate.
4. Select the option to submit.
5. Watch the cutscene — your hunter wakes in Yharnam as dawn breaks.

Thematically, this is the "walk away" ending. Your hunter escapes the dream and rejoins the waking world, but Yharnam is still Yharnam: the beast plague, the Healing Church, and the Great Ones remain exactly where they were. Community reports often describe this as the most humane ending and the most depressing one at the same time, since nothing about the city actually changes.

It's also the only ending that requires zero combat beyond the mandatory story bosses, making it a natural pick if you're underleveled or just want the credits.

## Ending 2 — Honoring Wishes (Refuse Gehrman)

Refuse Gehrman's offer and he stands up, draws the Burial Blade, and fights you himself. This is a real boss encounter, not a scripted loss — Gehrman is fast, punishes healing, and has a second phase that adds ranged pressure.

**What to expect:**

- Gehrman closes distance quickly and punishes greedy combos.
- His scythe swings have long reach, so dodge *through* the arc rather than backward.
- He can interrupt Blood Vial use, so heal only after a confirmed dodge or a stagger.
- Bring a fully upgraded weapon and a full stock of Blood Vials; there's no shortcut here.

Win, and the Moon Presence descends — but instead of fighting it, your hunter is embraced and bound to the Dream. You take Gehrman's place as the host, kneeling in the same spot, while the Doll tends to the workshop. It's a loop closing rather than breaking.

This ending is the default "refuse" outcome. It happens whether you've collected zero cords or two of them — you only need three to change the result.

## Ending 3 — Childhood's Beginning (The Umbilical Cord Route)

This is the true final boss route, and the one that requires planning. You need to consume **three** One Third of Umbilical Cord items before confronting Gehrman. There are four in the game, so you have one spare.

| Cord Source | Location | When It Becomes Available |
|---|---|---|
| Abandoned Old Workshop | Behind the locked door in Cathedral Ward, down to the old workshop | After defeating Blood-Starved Beast |
| Arianna's child | Oedon Chapel, speak with Arianna | After the Blood Moon (post-Rom) |
| Mergo's Wet Nurse | Nightmare of Mensis, after the boss falls | Immediately after the fight |
| Iosefka's Clinic | Return via the Forbidden Woods cave path | After the Blood Moon (post-Rom) |

**Order of operations:**

1. Progress to the Blood Moon (defeat Rom, the Vacuous Spider).
2. Collect Arianna's cord and the Iosefka's Clinic cord.
3. Defeat Mergo's Wet Nurse and grab the third cord.
4. Open your inventory and **use** three cords — this triggers a short vision and a stat bump.
5. Return to the Dream, refuse Gehrman, and defeat him.
6. When the Moon Presence appears, it will now be hostile. Fight and kill it.
7. Watch the ending: your hunter becomes an infant Great One, and the Doll cradles you.

The Moon Presence fight catches people off guard because it's the only time in the game you face a Great One in that form. It has an attack that drops you to a sliver of health, so keep healing topped off and don't trade hits.

## Bloodborne Endings Explained: Seeing All Three in One Playthrough

You don't need three full runs. Back up your save right before the final choice and reload it twice.

| Step | Action | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Defeat Mergo's Wet Nurse and collect all cords | All three endings now reachable |
| 2 | Upload your save to PS Plus cloud storage or a USB drive | Safe checkpoint |
| 3 | Reload, submit to Gehrman | Yharnam Sunrise |
| 4 | Restore the backup, refuse, beat Gehrman | Honoring Wishes |
| 5 | Restore again, consume 3 cords, refuse, beat Gehrman and Moon Presence | Childhood's Beginning |

Two practical tips: don't consume the cords until you're certain, because the item is finite, and don't restore a backup made *after* you've already triggered an ending — the game autosaves aggressively. On PS5, Bloodborne runs through backward compatibility, so cloud saves work the same way they did on PS4. For official details on the game itself, see the [PlayStation Bloodborne page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/bloodborne/).

## What the Endings Actually Mean

This is where Bloodborne endings explained turns into speculation, because the game deliberately withholds answers. Community reports and long-running fan theories tend to converge on a few readings:

- **Yharnam Sunrise** reads as ignorance being mercy. You escape, but you learn nothing and change nothing.
- **Honoring Wishes** reads as perpetuation. Gehrman was trapped as the Dream's host; now you are. The cycle continues with a new caretaker.
- **Childhood's Beginning** reads as transcendence. Rather than serving a Great One, you become one — small, helpless, and growing, with the Doll as your caretaker.

Player experience notes that the Doll's final gesture in Ending 3 mirrors how she treats you throughout the game, which is why many read it as the "good" ending despite its body horror. Others argue the opposite: that becoming a Great One means losing your humanity entirely. The game never picks a side, which is exactly why people are still arguing about it a decade later.

One thing is consistent across every theory: the cords represent insight you can't unlearn. Consuming them doesn't just unlock a boss — it changes what your hunter is capable of becoming.

## FAQ

**How many endings does Bloodborne have?**
Three. This Bloodborne endings explained guide covers all of them: Yharnam Sunrise, Honoring Wishes, and Childhood's Beginning.

**Do I need all four umbilical cords for the third ending?**
No. You only need to consume three. There are four available in a single playthrough, so missing one — usually Iosefka's Clinic — won't lock you out.

**Can I get all three endings in one playthrough?**
Yes. Back up your save after defeating Mergo's Wet Nurse but before speaking to Gehrman, then reload the backup between endings. Just don't consume the cords on the run where you want the first two endings.

**Does The Old Hunters DLC add a fourth ending?**
No. The expansion adds areas, weapons, and bosses, but the finale in the Hunter's Dream plays out exactly the same way.
