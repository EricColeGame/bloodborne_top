Few stat investments in Bloodborne reshape a hunter's toolkit as dramatically as Bloodtinge. A properly planned Bloodborne bloodtinge build turns your sidearm from a parry button into a real damage source and unlocks the Chikage's blood-soaked transformed mode, which shreds bosses and invaders alike once your stats are in place. This guide covers the stat targets, weapon picks, gem priorities, and progression route you need for a Bloodborne bloodtinge build that holds up in both PvE and PvP — including the pitfalls that make most first attempts feel weak around the mid-game.

## What Actually Scales With Bloodtinge

Bloodtinge is the stat that governs blood damage: firearm shots, the transformed modes of blood-imbued weapons, and a small handful of Hunter Tools. The key insight most players miss is that a bloodtinge build is not "high Bloodtinge and nothing else." You still need Vitality to survive and Skill to keep your untransformed weapon useful while you learn spacing.

Community testing has settled on two effective soft caps. From 1 to 25, every point of Bloodtinge delivers noticeable gains. From 25 to 50, returns stay strong but flatten slightly. Past 50, you are spending levels for scraps — usually better put into Vitality or Endurance.

| Bloodtinge Level | What It Unlocks | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 14 | Minimum to wield the Chikage | Entry point only |
| 18 | Evelyn becomes usable | Guns start hitting |
| 25 | First soft cap, guns feel real | Solid mid-game target |
| 40–50 | Near-maximum gun and blood damage | The endgame sweet spot |
| 50+ | Marginal gains per level | Stop here |

Player experience consistently backs this up: the difference between 25 and 40 Bloodtinge on an Evelyn shot is far larger than the difference between 40 and 50, so plan your level budget accordingly.

## Stat Spread, Origin, and Level Cap

Most bloodtinge build templates live between Blood Level 100 and 120, the range where the community PvP meta still finds matches. A common Skill/Bloodtinge template cited on the Bloodborne Wiki runs 25 Skill and 40–45 Bloodtinge, leaning on dodging, stagger pressure, and transformation (sheath) attacks rather than raw combo damage.

Here is a sample Blood Level 120 spread that leaves room for both weapons:

| Stat | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Vitality | 40–50 | Chikage drains HP; you need a buffer |
| Endurance | 20–25 | Enough for two combos plus a dodge |
| Strength | 10–14 | 14 opens the Bloodletter |
| Skill | 14–25 | 14 wields the Chikage; 25 boosts visceral damage |
| Bloodtinge | 40–50 | Your damage stat |
| Arcane | Base | Nothing here scales your blood damage |

For your starting origin, **Noble Scion** is the community favorite because it begins with the highest Bloodtinge of any origin, saving you a few levels. **Military Veteran** is the alternative if you want the Strength and Skill minimums for the Bloodletter without wasting points. **Waste of Skin** remains the min-maxer's pick when every single level matters.

One practical note: do not rush Bloodtinge to 50 before Vitality hits 30. New hunters who do this die to the Chikage's own health drain more often than to bosses.

## Best Weapons for a Bloodborne Bloodtinge Build

Your weapon choice defines the build. Blood-imbued weapons split their scaling: the normal mode scales with Skill and Strength, while the transformed mode converts to blood damage that scales almost entirely with Bloodtinge.

| Weapon | Commonly Cited Requirements | Why It Fits | Where to Find It |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chikage | 10 STR / 14 SKL / 14 BLT | Transformed mode deals pure blood damage off Bloodtinge | Chest inside Cainhurst Castle |
| Bloodletter | 14 STR / 14 SKL / 16 BLT | Huge blood damage and an AoE slam in tricked mode | Research Hall, The Old Hunters DLC |
| Simon's Bowblade | 8 STR / 15 SKL / 15 BLT | Charged arrows scale with Bloodtinge at range | Hunter's Nightmare, from Simon's questline |
| Saw Spear | Low requirements | Skill-scaling backup while you level Bloodtinge | Central Yharnam |

### The Chikage Is the Heart of the Build

The Chikage is the reason most players search for a Bloodborne bloodtinge build in the first place. In its normal form it is a fast, Skill-scaling katana. Trick it, and the blade coats in blood: damage now scales with Bloodtinge, hits harder, and steadily drains your health. The transform attack — the sheath draw — hits like a truck and is your best punish after a dodge. Treat the tricked state as a burst window, not a default stance, and sheath it the moment you need to heal.

### The Bloodletter Is the DLC Powerhouse

If you own The Old Hunters, the Bloodletter is the raw-damage counterpart. Its tricked form swings slower but deletes health bars, and its heavy blood attack costs HP to fire. Community reports describe it as the highest-ceiling bloodtinge weapon in the game, with the caveat that its self-damage punishes sloppy play. Pair it with high Vitality and you have a boss-killer.

### Don't Sleep on the Bowblade

Simon's Bowblade gives a bloodtinge build something it otherwise lacks: safe ranged damage. Charged shots from the bow form scale with Bloodtinge, letting you chip bosses from outside their swing range. It is slower to kill with than the Chikage, but it trivializes several fights that punish melee aggression.

## Firearms, Gems, and Runes

On a bloodtinge build, your gun is not a utility item — it is a second weapon. The Vileblood Handbook, a long-running community guide, calls one specific pistol the go-to for every bloodtinge setup, and the math agrees.

| Firearm | Strength | Weakness | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evelyn | Highest per-shot damage at high Bloodtinge | Single bullet, slow recovery | Cainhurst Castle |
| Repeating Pistol | Two-round burst for finishing | Consumes two bullets per shot | Forbidden Woods area |
| Hunter Pistol | Cheap, reliable, fast parry window | Low damage | Starting gift / Bath Messenger |
| Cannon | Massive burst | Heavy bullet cost, slow | Forbidden Woods |

**Gem priorities.** Community consensus holds that physical (Tempering) gems boost only your untransformed mode, while blood attack gems are what actually multiply Chikage and Bloodletter damage in tricked form. Farm blood attack gems in the Chalice Dungeons — Depth 5 roots with the right curse offer the biggest numbers — and accept a durability or stamina curse if it means a higher damage roll.

**Runes worth slotting:**

- **Formless Oedon** — more quicksilver bullets, which means more Evelyn shots.
- **Oedon Writhe** — restores bullets on visceral attacks, keeping your gun fed.
- **Clawmark** — boosts visceral damage, which your Skill investment already supports.
- **Communion** — extra blood vials to offset Chikage drain.

**Hunter Tools.** The Madaras Whistle scales with Bloodtinge and gives you a burst option when you are out of bullets; it is found in the Forbidden Woods. The Old Hunter Bone is a common inclusion on Skill/Bloodtinge templates for the faster dodge, which helps enormously when you are managing the Chikage's health drain mid-fight.

## Progression Roadmap

A bloodtinge build has an awkward early game because its best weapon is locked behind an optional area. Follow this order to avoid grinding.

| Step | Action | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick Noble Scion or Military Veteran | Highest starting Bloodtinge or clean weapon requirements |
| 2 | Level Vitality to 30 with Hunter Pistol and Saw Spear | Stay alive first; damage comes later |
| 3 | Reach the Forbidden Woods and grab the Cainhurst Summons | Found via the back entrance to Iosefka's Clinic |
| 4 | Take the carriage from Hemwick Charnel Lane to Cainhurst | Unlocks the Chikage and Evelyn |
| 5 | Push Bloodtinge to 25, then 40+ | Your damage finally arrives |
| 6 | Enter The Old Hunters for the Bloodletter and Bowblade | Optional but transformative |
| 7 | Farm blood attack gems in Depth 5 Chalice Dungeons | The last big damage jump |

If you are playing the base game only, steps 1–5 and 7 still deliver a complete Bloodborne bloodtinge build — you simply lean on the Chikage and Evelyn rather than the DLC weapons. For reference on the game itself, see the [official Bloodborne page on PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/bloodborne/).

## PvP and PvE Tactics That Win

Bloodtinge builds reward patience. Your damage is front-loaded into single heavy hits, so trading combos is a losing proposition.

- **Bait with the sheath attack.** Throw out a normal swing, then buffer the transform attack as your opponent closes. The Chikage's draw is one of the best roll-catches in the game.
- **Use Evelyn as a zoning tool.** At 40+ Bloodtinge, a pistol shot does meaningful chip damage and forces movement. Fire it during their heal, not randomly.
- **Respect your own health bar.** Both the Chikage and Bloodletter cost HP to use. Keep one blood vial in reserve specifically for the drain, and never enter a tricked state below half health.
- **Parry into visceral, then reposition.** Your Skill investment makes viscerals hit hard; the Oedon Writhe rune converts them into ammo.
- **Swap to the bow for slippery invaders.** Players who run from melee range often walk straight into charged Bowblade arrows.

In PvE, the pattern is simple: dodge, punish with a transform attack, sheath, and reset. Bosses with long recovery windows — most of the base game's larger enemies — fall quickly to this rhythm once your Bloodtinge passes 40.

## FAQ

**What is the best weapon for a first Bloodborne bloodtinge build?**
The Chikage. It requires only 14 Bloodtinge to wield, is available before the mid-game, and its transformed mode gives you the full blood damage experience without DLC. Add the Evelyn as soon as you reach Cainhurst and the build is functional.

**How much Bloodtinge do I actually need?**
Aim for 25 as your first milestone and 40–50 as your final target. Beyond 50, the returns are small enough that extra points are better spent on Vitality or Endurance.

**Does Bloodtinge increase visceral attack damage?**
Community testing attributes visceral damage primarily to Skill and character level, not Bloodtinge. That is why most bloodtinge build templates still push Skill to 25 rather than leaving it at the Chikage's minimum.

**Can I run this build without The Old Hunters DLC?**
Yes. The Chikage, Evelyn, and Madaras Whistle are all in the base game, and a Bloodtinge 50 Chikage with decent blood attack gems clears every boss in the main campaign. The Bloodletter and Simon's Bowblade simply add more options.
