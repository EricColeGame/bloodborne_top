Picking the wrong trick weapon can turn Bloodborne's opening hours into a grind, which is why so many hunters eventually go hunting for a ranked list. The Bloodborne best weapons aren't simply the ones with the biggest attack rating — moveset, stamina cost, scaling, and gem slots matter just as much as raw damage. This guide breaks down the Bloodborne best weapons by build, playstyle, and progression stage, so you can commit your Blood Stone Shards with confidence instead of second-guessing every upgrade.

## How We Ranked the Bloodborne Best Weapons

Ranking trick weapons is never purely a numbers game. A weapon with a massive attack rating can still feel miserable if its swings empty your stamina bar or its hitboxes whiff on tall bosses. Our shortlist weighs five factors:

- **Moveset flexibility** — how well the tricked and untricked forms handle crowds, single targets, and tight corridors
- **Scaling and build fit** — whether a weapon rewards Strength, Skill, Bloodtinge, Arcane, or a quality split
- **Stamina economy** — how many swings you get per bar at base Endurance
- **Gem potential** — how much damage a weapon gains from a well-rolled set of blood gems
- **Accessibility** — when you can actually obtain it, and how much of the early game it carries you through

Community reports and years of accumulated player experience line up closely with these criteria, which is why the same handful of names keeps resurfacing in every discussion thread. That consensus is a useful starting point, but the honest answer is that the best weapon is the one whose rhythm matches your dodges.

## Starting Weapons: Saw Cleaver vs. Hunter Axe vs. Threaded Cane

Your first choice matters more than it looks, because all three starter weapons can carry a full playthrough if you build around them.

| Weapon | Trick form | Key perk | Watch out for |
|--------|-----------|----------|---------------|
| Saw Cleaver | Folding saw | Serrated bonus, cheap transform attacks | Short reach in close quarters |
| Hunter Axe | Two-handed halberd | Charged R2 spin, excellent rally | Slow swings against fast enemies |
| Threaded Cane | Bladed whip | Serrated whip form, righteous cane form | Low base damage early on |

### Saw Cleaver

The safest recommendation for a newcomer. Fast light attacks, low stamina costs, and a beast-killing serrated edge make it forgiving without being weak. It rewards aggressive play and pairs naturally with Fire Paper against furry nightmares.

### Hunter Axe

The axe trades speed for reach and stagger. Its charged two-handed R2 spins through groups, and its high rally potential means you can trade hits and heal much of the damage back. It's also one of the strongest Strength weapons available before the mid-game.

### Threaded Cane

The cane is the connoisseur's pick. The whip form adds serrated damage against beasts and covers a wide arc, while the cane form carries a righteous bonus that helps against certain corrupted enemies. It scales with Skill, so it suits players who prefer mobility and precise spacing over brute force.

## Late-Game and DLC Standouts

Several of the Bloodborne best weapons live behind the Old Hunters expansion, and they're worth the trip.

### Ludwig's Holy Blade

Available once you've picked up the Radiant Sword Hunter Badge, this is the classic quality weapon: a straightforward longsword that becomes a massive greatsword. It scales well with both Strength and Skill, hits like a truck, and has one of the most reliable movesets in the game.

### Whirligig Saw

A Strength monster from the DLC. Its L2 revs the saw and melts the health bars of large, slow bosses, especially once you've staggered them. Add serrated damage and it becomes a beast-killer with few equals.

### Chikage

The signature Bloodtinge weapon. In its tricked form it deals blood damage that scales purely off Bloodtinge, at the cost of draining your own health. It's a high-risk, high-reward pick that demands a committed build.

### Rakuyo

A Skill weapon with almost no downtime — its combos flow into transform attacks that keep pressure on without draining stamina. Getting it means surviving a notoriously nasty DLC encounter, but the payoff is one of the smoothest movesets FromSoftware has designed.

## Matching the Bloodborne Best Weapons to Your Build

Because Bloodborne has no respec, your stat spread locks in your options. Plan around a target weapon before you spend echoes.

| Build | Priority stats | Top picks | Notes |
|-------|---------------|-----------|-------|
| Strength | Strength, Vitality | Hunter Axe, Whirligig Saw, Kirkhammer | Heavy stagger, slow swings |
| Skill | Skill, Vitality, Endurance | Rakuyo, Blade of Mercy, Church Pick | Fast, dodge-focused |
| Quality | Strength + Skill | Saw Cleaver, Ludwig's Holy Blade | Most flexible for a first run |
| Bloodtinge | Bloodtinge, Vitality | Chikage, Simon's Bowblade, Evelyn | Expensive to build, very strong late |
| Arcane | Arcane, Vitality | Holy Moonlight Sword, Tonitrus, Logarius' Wheel | Tool usage matters as much as the weapon |

A practical rule: pick one primary weapon and one backup that covers its weakness. A Skill build running Rakuyo, for example, benefits from a thrust-heavy option for enemies that resist slashing.

## Firearms: The Other Half of the Loadout

Your left hand isn't just for parries. Firearms control spacing, interrupt attacks, and set up visceral attacks — and the right one changes your whole approach.

| Firearm | Requires | Strength | Best for |
|---------|----------|----------|----------|
| Hunter's Pistol | Low Bloodtinge | Reliable, fast parry windows | Almost every build |
| Evelyn | High Bloodtinge | Best damage scaling of the pistols | Bloodtinge builds |
| Blunderbuss | Low Bloodtinge | Wide spread, easy point-blank parries | Crowd control and panic parries |
| Cannon | Strength | Massive burst damage | Finishing off staggered bosses |
| Ludwig's Rifle | Moderate Bloodtinge | Long range, tight spread | Pulling and poking |

For most players, the Hunter's Pistol is the default because its parry timing is generous and its range is honest. The Evelyn becomes the clear winner once your Bloodtinge is high enough to justify it, and community reports regularly call it the strongest firearm in the game for that reason.

## Upgrade Priorities, Gems, and Practical Tips

Weapon upgrades matter more than levels in the early game. Fortifying a weapon to +3 or +4 will do more for your damage than three points of Strength.

| Stage | Material | Where it comes from | Priority |
|-------|----------|--------------------|----------|
| +1 to +3 | Blood Stone Shards | Early enemies, Bath Messenger | Upgrade your main weapon first |
| +4 to +6 | Twin Blood Stone Shards | Mid-game areas, messengers | Push to +6 before Vicar Amelia |
| +7 to +9 | Blood Stone Chunks | Late areas, chalice dungeons | Save for your endgame weapon |
| +10 | Blood Rock | Limited finds per playthrough | One weapon only — choose carefully |

A few habits that separate smooth runs from frustrating ones:

- **Learn the transform attack.** Pressing L1 mid-combo is free damage on almost every weapon and often staggers enemies out of their wind-up.
- **Match damage types to enemies.** Beasts hate serrated weapons and fire; kin hate thrust and bolt. Swapping papers is cheaper than swapping weapons.
- **Don't neglect rally.** Bloodborne rewards aggression — hitting back after taking damage recovers health, and high-rally weapons like the Hunter Axe make this a real defensive tool.
- **Check gem slots before you commit.** Uncanny and Lost variants of the same weapon offer different slot layouts, and a good gem set can outweigh a whole upgrade level.

## FAQ

**What is the best weapon in Bloodborne for a first playthrough?**
The Saw Cleaver. It's available at the start, cheap on stamina, strong against beasts, and it never stops being useful. If you prefer reach and stagger, the Hunter Axe is an equally valid pick.

**Are the Bloodborne best weapons different for PvP and PvE?**
Yes. PvE favors high damage-per-swing and serrated bonuses against beasts, while PvP rewards fast weapons with unpredictable transform attacks — the Rakuyo and Blade of Mercy are far more popular in duels than their raw numbers suggest.

**Do I need the Old Hunters DLC to get the strongest weapons?**
Not strictly, but several of the Bloodborne best weapons — the Whirligig Saw, Rakuyo, Holy Moonlight Sword, Church Pick, and Simon's Bowblade — are found in the expansion. The base game still offers Ludwig's Holy Blade, Chikage, and the Saw Cleaver.

**Can I respec my stats if I change my mind?**
No. Bloodborne has no respec system, so decide on a build direction early and let your weapon choice guide your level-ups. A quality build using Strength and Skill is the most forgiving starting point if you're undecided.
