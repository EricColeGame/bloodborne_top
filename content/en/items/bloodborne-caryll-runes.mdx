## What Are Caryll Runes in Bloodborne?

Few upgrades in Bloodborne change a hunt as quietly as Caryll runes. They don't glow, they don't drop from bosses with a fanfare, and the game barely explains them — yet three well-chosen runes can mean the difference between a comfortable boss attempt and a one-way trip back to the lamp. Bloodborne Caryll runes are etched symbols worn by the hunter, transcribed from the "formless sounds" of the Great Ones by a scholar named Caryll, and each one bends a specific rule of combat in your favor.

Unlike blood gems, which are slotted into individual weapons, Bloodborne Caryll runes attach to your character and stay with you permanently. Once you pick one up, it's yours forever — through deaths, through New Game Plus, through every chalice dive you'll ever attempt. The only limit is how many you can wear at once, and that limit is three.

That small number is exactly why the system matters. With only three slots, every rune is a tradeoff, and the "best" setup depends entirely on whether you're farming echoes, learning a boss, or pushing into a Depth 5 root chalice.

| Rune Family | Examples | What It Changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attribute | Clockwise / Anti-Clockwise Metamorphosis | Max HP, max stamina |
| Resource | Communion, Formless Oedon | Blood vial and quicksilver bullet capacity |
| Economy | Moon, Heir, Eye | Blood echo gain, item discovery |
| Visceral | Clawmark, Blood Rapture, Oedon Writhe | Visceral attack damage, healing, bullet refunds |
| Defense | Great Lake, Arcane Lake, Deep Sea, Fading/Dissipating Lake | Damage type and status resistances |
| Covenant | Radiance, Corruption, Impurity, Hunter of Hunters | Multiplayer matching and covenant behavior |
| Transformation | Beast's Embrace, Milkweed (The Old Hunters) | Alters your hunter's body and moveset |

## How to Unlock and Equip Caryll Runes

You can collect runes long before you can use them, which trips up a lot of first-time hunters. Equipping requires the **Rune Workshop Tool**, a key item tucked away in the side area of **Hemwick Charnel Lane**, branching off Cathedral Ward. It's easy to walk past, and plenty of players finish their first playthrough with a bag full of runes they never equipped.

Once you have the tool, an altar in the Hunter's Dream becomes active. Interact with it to open the rune menu and slot up to three runes. Swapping is free, instant, and reversible — there's no cost, no consumable, and no penalty for experimenting. If a boss is shredding you, go back to the dream, change your loadout, and try again.

A few practical notes that aren't obvious from the menu:

- Runes are **never consumed or lost**, so pick up every glowing corpse item you find.
- Runes carry into **New Game Plus**, and duplicate tiers stack in your inventory as separate entries.
- Runes do **not** affect weapons — that's the blood gem system, managed at the workshop.
- Covenant runes occupy a slot too, so wearing one is a real build decision, not a free bonus.

| Step | Action | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reach the side path off Cathedral Ward | Cathedral Ward |
| 2 | Explore Hemwick Charnel Lane and pick up the Rune Workshop Tool | Hemwick Charnel Lane |
| 3 | Return to the Hunter's Dream and use the altar | Hunter's Dream |
| 4 | Slot up to three runes and swap freely any time | Hunter's Dream |

## The Best Caryll Runes by Category

Because slots are scarce, it helps to think in families rather than a single tier list. The values below are approximate and rise with each tier of the same rune.

**Attribute and resource runes** are the safest picks in the game. More HP, more stamina, more vials, and more bullets are universally useful and never wasted.

| Rune | Effect (Approx.) | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clockwise Metamorphosis | Max HP +5% / +10% / +15% | Every build, especially new hunters |
| Anti-Clockwise Metamorphosis | Max stamina +10% / +15% / +20% | Aggressive play, heavy weapons |
| Communion | Max blood vials +1 to +4 | Long chalice runs, boss learning |
| Formless Oedon | Max quicksilver bullets +1 to +4 | Arcane tools, firearm-focused builds |

**Economy and visceral runes** are where the real specialization happens. Moon and Heir turn a grind into a breeze, while Clawmark and its cousins reward players who live for the parry-then-riposte rhythm.

| Rune | Effect (Approx.) | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moon | More blood echoes from defeated enemies | Leveling, blood echo farming |
| Heir | More blood echoes from visceral attacks | Parry-heavy playstyles |
| Eye | Raises item discovery | Farming chunks and ritual materials |
| Clawmark | Boosts visceral attack damage | Riposte and backstab builds |
| Blood Rapture | Visceral attacks restore HP | Sustain without vial spam |
| Oedon Writhe | Visceral attacks grant quicksilver bullets | Arcane and gun-focused builds |

**Defensive runes** are the most underrated group. Flat damage reduction sounds boring until you're eating a Winter Lantern's gaze in the Nightmare of Mensis or a hunter's rifle in PvP.

| Rune | Resistance |
| --- | --- |
| Great Lake | Physical damage reduction |
| Arcane Lake | Arcane damage reduction |
| Fading Lake | Fire damage reduction |
| Dissipating Lake | Bolt damage reduction |
| Deep Sea | Frenzy resistance |
| Clear Deep Sea | Rapid poison resistance |
| Stunning Deep Sea | Slow poison resistance |

Player experience is nearly unanimous here: a Deep Sea rune makes the frenzy-heavy sections of the Nightmare realms and the Research Hall dramatically more forgiving, especially on a low-Insight character.

## Where to Find the Most Useful Bloodborne Caryll Runes

Some of the strongest early runes come from questlines and slightly hidden corners rather than bosses. The table below covers a few confirmed, high-value pickups.

| Rune | Tier | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anti-Clockwise Metamorphosis | +10% stamina | Forbidden Woods — on a corpse near the blue, alien-like enemies |
| Anti-Clockwise Metamorphosis | +15% stamina | Lecture Building — trade with Patches the Spider via the second-floor ladder (either dialogue option works) |
| Heir | +20% echoes from viscerals | Tomb of Oedon — dropped by Henryk; follow Eileen the Crow's questline to trigger the fight |
| Heir | +40% echoes from viscerals | Yahar'gul, Unseen Village — corpse in the bell-ringer room near a brick troll |

Community reports also mention a rally-potential rune that exists in the game's data with no legitimate drop location, obtainable only through save editing. Treat it as cut content and don't plan a build around it.

Covenant runes work differently. Radiance, Corruption, Impurity, and Hunter of Hunters are handed out by covenant NPCs, and their value is mostly about online behavior — who can summon you, who you invade, and what rewards you earn from kills. The Old Hunters expansion added Beast's Embrace and Milkweed in the Research Hall, both of which physically transform your hunter and change how the character plays.

### Farming High-Tier Runes in Chalice Dungeons

The strongest versions of most runes drop from chalice dungeon bosses and depth-based loot, not the main campaign. A few guidelines from the community:

- **Depth 5 root chalices** with Fetid, Rotted, and Cursed rites produce the highest-tier rune drops.
- Layer bosses are the most reliable source; check every sarcophagus room before you leave a layer.
- Shared glyphs let you repeatedly farm a specific boss — search for a glyph tied to the rune you want rather than clearing random dungeons.
- Bring Communion and Clockwise Metamorphosis while you farm; extra vials and HP save far more time than extra echoes.

## FAQ

**Do Bloodborne Caryll runes carry over to New Game Plus?**
Yes. Every rune you collect is permanent and stays in your inventory across playthroughs. Only your progress flags and key items reset, so a rune found in a first playthrough remains usable in NG+ and beyond.

**Can you equip more than three Caryll runes at once?**
No. The Rune Workshop Tool unlocks three slots at the altar in the Hunter's Dream, and that's the cap for the entire game, including The Old Hunters expansion.

**Do Caryll runes affect my weapons?**
No. Weapons are customized with blood gems at the workshop. Bloodborne Caryll runes apply to your hunter's stats, resistances, and visceral attacks, which is why they pair so well with gem builds rather than replacing them.

**What's the best Caryll rune for a beginner?**
Clockwise Metamorphosis. A flat percentage boost to max HP helps in every single encounter, costs you nothing to swap out later, and is one of the easiest runes to find early. Pair it with Communion and you'll survive long enough to actually learn boss patterns.

**Are covenant runes worth a slot?**
Only if you're engaging with multiplayer. Radiance, Corruption, Impurity, and Hunter of Hunters change who you can summon or invade and what rewards you get. In pure single-player, a stat or defense rune will always do more for you.
