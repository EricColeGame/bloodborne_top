Eileen the Crow is the one NPC in Bloodborne who treats hunters as prey, and her storyline is also one of the easiest in the game to break by accident. Miss a single conversation or wander into the Forbidden Woods too early, and the Bloodborne Eileen quest quietly collapses — no badge, no rune, and sometimes an angry crow waiting at the Grand Cathedral instead. This guide walks through every trigger, every reward, and every fail state so you can finish the Bloodborne Eileen quest in a single playthrough without losing her.

## Who Is Eileen the Crow in Bloodborne?

Eileen is a Hunter of Hunters: an assassin who tracks down hunters who have lost themselves to bloodlust or begun turning into beasts. She wears a beaked mask packed with incense to mask the smell of blood, and a crowfeather cape that nods to sky burial rites from her homeland.

Community lore discussion and item descriptions both point to her being a foreigner — the Crow Hunter Badge calls her an "outsider from the hinterlands," the only mention of that region in the game. She also knows about the Hunter's Dream and the Plain Doll, which strongly suggests she was once a dream-bound hunter like you before breaking free.

What makes her quest worth doing:

- **Crow Hunter Badge** — unlocks her armor set and the Blade of Mercy for purchase.
- **Hunter Caryll rune** — a strong rune reward for finishing her storyline.
- **Hunter of Hunters covenant** — a unique oath rune option.
- **Four gestures** — Shake Off Cape, Shhh!, Approval, and Wait.
- **A genuinely great character arc** — one of the few NPCs in Yharnam whose fate you can meaningfully change.

## Where to Find Eileen: All Quest Locations

Eileen appears in four distinct places, and each one is tied to a progression gate. The table below summarizes them in the order most players hit them.

| Stage | Location | How to Reach It | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rafters above the Central Yharnam sewers | From the Central Yharnam lamp, cross the bridge past the caged dogs, drop through the barrel-blocked window, then enter the second barrel-blocked window on the right wall | Available from the start |
| 2 | Cathedral Ward, just outside the main door | Exit the Cathedral Ward lamp doorway and turn hard left along the railing | Requires the large circular gate and the lever-operated side gate to be opened |
| 3 | Tomb of Oedon | Travel from the Central Yharnam or Cathedral Ward lamp; the Tomb lamp is unusable during this event | Speak to her in Cathedral Ward first |
| 4 | Top of the Grand Cathedral steps | Walk up the main approach to the Grand Cathedral | After defeating Rom, the Vacuous Spider |

Two quick warnings from player experience: killing Darkbeast Paarl can make Eileen vanish from Cathedral Ward, and entering the Forbidden Woods before the Tomb of Oedon fight ends her quest entirely.

## Bloodborne Eileen Quest Walkthrough, Step by Step

The Bloodborne Eileen quest runs on a strict order of operations. Use this checklist as your progress tracker.

| Step | Action | Reward | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Talk to Eileen twice at the Central Yharnam rafters | 4× Bold Hunter's Mark + Shake Off Cape gesture | Before the Blood Moon |
| 2 | Open the gates, then speak with her outside Cathedral Ward | Shhh! gesture + Henryk warning | Before examining the skull after Vicar Amelia |
| 3 | Help her defeat Henryk at the Tomb of Oedon | Approval gesture + Heir Caryll Rune | Before entering the Forbidden Woods |
| 4 | After Rom, speak to her at the Grand Cathedral steps | Wait gesture | Before the final boss sequence |
| 5 | Defeat the Bloody Crow of Cainhurst inside the Grand Cathedral | Crow Hunter Badge + Hunter Caryll rune | Endgame |

### Step 1: The Sewer Rafters

The first meeting is easy to miss because the entrance hides behind stacked boxes and barrels near a staircase close to the old lady's lamp. Speak to her twice — the second conversation is what hands over the gesture and the Bold Hunter's Marks.

### Step 2: Cathedral Ward

You must open all three gates in the plaza before she appears: the big gate toward the Cathedral, and the smaller gate opened with a lever after climbing the stairs and turning right. Then walk out of the lamp doorway and look immediately left. She warns you away from the tomb below Oedon Chapel — a warning you should absolutely ignore.

### Step 3: The Tomb of Oedon

Approach the tomb and Henryk spawns. Eileen joins the fight automatically once you engage. Kill Henryk, and she hands over the Approval gesture. Her corpse-adjacent dialogue if she dies here is one of the saddest lines in the game, so keep her alive.

### Step 4 and 5: The Grand Cathedral

After Rom, she's injured at the top of the steps. Speak to her, take the Wait gesture, and enter the Grand Cathedral to fight the Bloody Crow of Cainhurst alone — no summons allowed. Win, and she gives you the Crow Hunter Badge and Hunter rune before resting.

## How to Help Eileen Fight Henryk Without Turning Her Hostile

Henryk is a fast, aggressive old hunter with a threaded cane and a chip-damage moveset. The real danger isn't him — it's clipping Eileen with your own attacks. Hit her too many times during the brawl and she stays hostile even after you reload the area, permanently breaking the quest.

Practical tips from community reports:

- **Bring Poison Knives.** They're found in the alley near Arianna's window. Poison lets Henryk die without you needing to swing near Eileen.
- **Fight from range.** Threaded Cane whip form, Hunter's Axe extended, or any thrusting weapon keeps your hitbox away from her.
- **Don't panic-heal into her.** Backing up while chugging vials is how most accidental hits happen.
- **If Henryk kills you but poison finishes him as you die,** he respawns and the fight resets. Make sure the poison tick lands while you're still standing.

Player experience note: the Henryk encounter can still be done during the night phase after examining the skull. The truly time-sensitive part is the Cathedral Ward conversation itself.

## Beating the Bloody Crow of Cainhurst: Strategies That Still Work

The final encounter inside the Grand Cathedral is widely considered one of the hardest NPC fights in Bloodborne. He hits like a truck, parries constantly, and heals. The old cheese strategy was patched out in version 1.04, so you need one of these approaches instead.

| Strategy | How It Works | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|
| Chikage drain | Bait his transformed Chikage, break line of sight near the entrance stairs, and let the weapon's HP drain kill him | Low |
| Poison Knife leash | Aggro him, run to the doors, and poison him each time his AI walks him back to spawn | Low–Medium |
| Out-range pokes | Hunter's Axe or Threaded Cane extended attacks from the doorway; he won't leave the building | Medium |
| Rifle Spear charge | Back away until he walks straight at you, then land a charged strong attack that staggers him | Medium–High |
| Straight-up duel | Parry practice, pistol chip, and dodge discipline | High |

Do **not** use the Choir Bell on the injured Eileen outside the cathedral. Community reports confirm it kills her outright and locks you out of the rewards.

Failure states to avoid:

## FAQ

**Can I still complete the Bloodborne Eileen quest after defeating Rom?**
Only if you already helped her at the Tomb of Oedon. If you spoke to her at least once but skipped Henryk, she becomes hostile at the Grand Cathedral after Rom and the quest is unrecoverable in that run.

**What happens if Eileen dies during the Henryk fight?**
The quest ends immediately. You can still loot Henryk's Heir Caryll Rune from his corpse, but you'll never get the Approval gesture, the Crow Hunter Badge from her, or access to the Hunter of Hunters covenant.

**Does killing Eileen break anything else?**
You lose the Hunter Caryll Rune, the gestures, and the covenant oath, but the Crow Hunter Badge still drops, so her armor and Blade of Mercy remain purchasable. It's a valid shortcut if you only want the gear.

**Is the Bloodborne Eileen quest worth doing on every playthrough?**
For most players, yes. The Hunter rune and covenant oath are genuinely useful, the Blade of Mercy is a strong skill-scaling weapon, and the quest takes under an hour once you know the route. Just clear Henryk before you ever set foot in the Forbidden Woods.
