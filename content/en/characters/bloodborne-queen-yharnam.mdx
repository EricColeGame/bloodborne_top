Most hunters finish Bloodborne without ever meeting the woman the city is named after. Bloodborne Queen Yharnam sits at the bottom of the Great Pthumeru Ihyll Chalice Dungeon, behind hours of ritual farming, depth-5 enemies, and one of the longest health bars in the game. She is also the only boss in the base game tied to a gold trophy, which makes her a bucket-list kill for completionists. This guide to Bloodborne Queen Yharnam covers where to find her, every attack in her kit, phase-by-phase counters, and the rewards waiting at the end.

## Who Is Queen Yharnam in Bloodborne?

Yharnam, Pthumerian Queen is the ruler of the ancient Pthumerian civilization that built the labyrinth beneath the city. In the main story she appears only as a silent, weeping apparition in Mergo's Loft, cradling a child and fading away when you approach — a cameo most players never connect to a boss fight. The real encounter with Bloodborne Queen Yharnam happens in the Great Pthumeru Ihyll Chalice Dungeon, where she fights with blood magic, a ritual knife, and a health pool that punishes sloppy aggression.

She is entirely optional. Nothing on the main path requires you to fight her, and the chalice dungeons are a separate progression track with their own difficulty curve. For trophy hunters, though, she matters: defeating her is the only gold trophy awarded for a boss in Bloodborne.

## How to Reach Queen Yharnam in the Chalice Dungeons

Reaching Bloodborne Queen Yharnam is a marathon, not a sprint. Story chalices must be cleared in order, because each one drops the chalice required to create the next. You cannot skip ahead on a first playthrough, so plan for a full dungeon run rather than a single boss attempt.

| Step | Chalice Dungeon | Depth | What It Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pthumeru Chalice | 1 | Central Pthumeru Chalice |
| 2 | Central Pthumeru | 2 | Lower Pthumeru Chalice |
| 3 | Lower Pthumeru | 3 | Defiled Chalice + Root variants |
| 4 | Defiled Chalice | 4 | Great Pthumeru Ihyll Chalice |
| 5 | Great Pthumeru Ihyll | 5 | Queen Yharnam boss fight |

Materials are the real bottleneck. Creating depth-4 and depth-5 dungeons demands Ritual Blood (5), Arcane Haze, Bastards of Loran, and Yellow Backbones in steady supply. Two shortcuts save hours of grinding:

- **Arcane Haze:** after clearing Lower Pthumeru, create a Lower Pthumeru Root Chalice and hunt for the Workshop Haze Extractor, which lets you convert spare ritual materials into Arcane Haze instead of farming it.
- **Ritual Blood (5):** search for a Standard, Loran, Depth 4 dungeon, run past the first room's enemies to the ladder, cross the blade-trap tunnel, and loot the chest past the werewolf. Reset the dungeon and repeat.

Community reports also point to shared glyph codes that speed up co-op and material runs — for example, the Great Pthumeru Ihyll glyph **egn5rrdj** is widely passed around by players. Treat any glyph you find online as player experience rather than gospel, since availability depends on your progress and online connection.

## Queen Yharnam Attacks and How to Counter Them

Every blood-based attack in this fight builds rapid poison, so antidotes are not optional. The table below breaks down her full kit and the cleanest response to each move, based on community testing and repeated player experience.

| Attack | What It Looks Like | How to Counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blood Missile | One fast projectile early, three later | Dodge left or right; clones only fire one shot, which identifies them |
| Blood Fountain | Slow rain of blood from her wrists | Quickstep through it toward her in phase 1; back off once phase 2 starts |
| Blood Blast | Hands raised, close-range AoE burst | Dodge straight backward, then quickstep back in for a hit |
| Telekinesis | She bows, ground glows blue | Sprint out of the glowing border or rush in and interrupt her |
| Single Blood Spear | She stabs her hand, a spear erupts under you | Dodge the moment she stabs herself — not before |
| Wall of Blood Spears | A line of spears rises from the floor | Quickstep behind her for a charged backstab |
| Sword Rush | Long leaping slash | Easily sidestepped; punish the recovery |
| Blood Storm | Sword planted, blood pillars erupt | Do not trade — dodge away and wait it out |
| Sword Combo | Slashes and stabs with her knife or blade | Bait a swing, dodge back, and punish the follow-up with a parry |

## Phase-by-Phase Strategy for Bloodborne Queen Yharnam

The fight is less about reflexes and more about respecting a rhythm set by the baby's crying. When the infant wails, you get rooted in place — and being rooted mid-combo is how most attempts end.

| Phase | Health Range | New Behavior | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | 100–75% | Blood missiles, blood fountain, AoE blast | Stay close, land 1–2 hits, reset |
| Phase 2 | 75–50% | Handcuffs break, knife melee begins | Farm parries for Visceral Attacks |
| Phase 3 | Below 50% | Ground spears, clones, Blood Storm | Play patient, use throwables |

### Phase 1: Punish the Silence

In the opening phase you can be surprisingly greedy. Community reports note that if the baby stays quiet, you can chain light attacks and interrupt her wind-ups before they finish. Listen for the cry, though — only the tail end of the *second* cry roots you at melee range, so jump back when you hear it and step in again after. Quickstepping through the blood fountain works here and stops working the moment phase 2 begins.

### Phase 2: Break Her Handcuffs, Then Break Her Posture

Around 70–75% she snaps her handcuffs and starts swinging a knife, which is the best news of the fight. Knife swings are generous parry windows. Step in briefly, backstep to a safe distance, and fire your gun on the follow-through — parrying from too close gets you chained and denies the Visceral Attack. Poison Knives are excellent here: roughly four will proc slow poison, chipping away at a health bar that otherwise feels endless.

### Phase 3: Manage the Arena

Below 50% she adds ground spears, a clone summon, and Blood Storm. Clones die to a single hit or gunshot, so clear them immediately. For the spear waves, sidestep the fast ones, sprint around the arena, and remember the sixth spear is delayed. Throwing any knife at her briefly staggers her, which buys free healing windows and forces melee attempts you can parry.

## Best Weapons, Items, and Build Tips

Loadout matters more than raw damage here. The right tools shorten the fight dramatically; the wrong ones turn it into a poison-management sim.

| Tool | Why It Works |
| --- | --- |
| Antidotes (5–10) | Counters rapid poison from every blood attack |
| Poison Knives (10–20) | Four procs slow poison; chip damage adds up fast |
| Throwing Knives | Staggers her, interrupts attacks, opens heal windows |
| Transformed Threaded Cane | Long reach hits without triggering the baby's cry |
| Holy Moonlight Sword (L2) | Repeated knockdowns; beware clipping her into scenery |
| Any firearm | Core parry tool for phase 2 and 3 Visceral Attacks |

A few practical habits separate a clean kill from a 20-minute war of attrition:

- Cap your combos at two hits unless the baby is silent.
- Keep the arena center clear — walls and the central column cause clipping and lost damage windows.
- Save your Poison Knives for the second half, when her health bar stops melting.
- Equip poison-resistance runes and attire if you find yourself healing more than attacking.

## Rewards, Trophy, and Notes

Killing Bloodborne Queen Yharnam in the Great Pthumeru Ihyll Dungeon awards the Yharnam Stone and the game's only gold boss trophy. The Mergo's Loft apparition is separate — she drops nothing there, and she still appears in the main story even after you've killed her in the chalice dungeon.

## FAQ

**Is Bloodborne Queen Yharnam required to finish the game?**
No. She is a completely optional chalice dungeon boss. You can finish the main story without ever setting foot in the Great Pthumeru Ihyll Dungeon, though you'll miss the only gold boss trophy in the game.

**What level should I be for Bloodborne Queen Yharnam?**
Most players tackle her somewhere in the 100–130 range with a fully upgraded weapon, since depth-5 dungeons scale hard. If your weapon is below +8, farm upgrade materials before creating the Ihyll chalice.

**Can you parry Queen Yharnam?**
Yes — she is one of the most parry-friendly bosses in Bloodborne. Her knife combos in phase 2 and 3 have wide windows, and Visceral Attacks are the fastest way to burn through her health bar.

**Does she drop anything in Mergo's Loft?**
No. The weeping apparition in Mergo's Loft is story-only. The Yharnam Stone and the trophy come exclusively from defeating Bloodborne Queen Yharnam in the Great Pthumeru Ihyll Chalice Dungeon.
